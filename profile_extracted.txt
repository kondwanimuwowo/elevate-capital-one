business ideas and the much needed systems
that help business thrive and scale.
We exist to make access to funding easier while
protecting value for investors and stakeholders.
Simply put, Elevate was created to ensure that
potential doesn’t go unrealized.

Elevate Capital structures select investments as
convertible instruments, where funding is initially
provided as debt with the option to convert into
equity at a future date.
Convertible investments are typically governed by
predefined terms, including conversion triggers,
valuation benchmarks, and timelines. These may
be linked to revenue growth, follow-on funding
rounds, or strategic inflection points within the
business.
This 	model 	offers 	flexibility 	for 	founders 	by
reducing 	early-stage 	dilution, 	while 	providing
Elevate Capital with capital protection through
debt 	characteristics 	and 	the 	potential 	to
participate 	in 	long-term 	equity 	upside 	upon
conversion.
